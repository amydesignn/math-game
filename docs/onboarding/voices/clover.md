# Clover (kid copy, QA, edge cases)

- **About + 3 steps, ending on the garden with "Let's play →".** I agree with Nathan's shape. On the words I side with Kestrel: line 1 is the loop ("Solve math to earn gems 💎"), because a 7-year-old can't act on "a gym for your brain". The gym line is lovely, but it's a parent line, so it goes in the small print.
- **An edge case nobody named: "You're the Sorcerer" goes false from Settings.** A kid who swapped to Sunny or Bookworm, or bought the Sproutling, opens How to Play and is told she's the Sorcerer with a cat. Amy's own wording fixes it: "You **start** as the Sorcerer…". Every line has to stay true for a returning kid, not just on day 1.
- **I'm with Rowan, against "the hints will teach it".** Even with `HINTS_ON` on, nothing teaches zoom, the 🛍️ shop or watering. So the shop goes into the copy and the pictures (Linden's icon-matching point), and I'd add Kestrel's "Pinch to see more". In the playtest, zoom was the biggest blocker: 1.5 min stuck before anything to do was even on screen.
- **A truth catch on the images:** the 4 files aren't Oscar's art. They're in-game captures (Nathan's canvas grabs for steps 1–2, Amy's shots for 3–4). Oscar made the card. Step 2 already shows the teal gem in its ring, so keep it. Step 1 hides her behind a tree, so it needs a new capture. The canvas-grab trick is HUD-free, so it **can't** show the 🛍️/🧺 icons Linden wants. Those need a full-page shot.
- **A kid taps outside.** On first run, one tap on the blurry Door behind the card ("I want that world!") dismisses the whole onboarding at word zero, and it never comes back on its own. Amy chose scrim-dismiss because players felt trapped, and the ✕ solved "trapped". I'd keep the ✕ and let the scrim close only from Settings.
- **QA I'll run:** both entries at 375 / 768 / 812×375 landscape, a fast-Next stale-image check, reload mid-first-run, reduced motion, 0 oscillators, and Halloween arriving only after the first world exit.
- **Changed my mind after Heron:** the gym line leaves the game altogether. "Teaches the method" is the true *and* different line for a parent, and the grade line should be built from the code's `grade` tags, not typed.
