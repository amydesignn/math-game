# Fern (motion)

- **The open question:** I agree with Nathan: About + 3 steps, ending on the garden step with "Let's play". Fewer slides means less motion to get through. The images do the teaching, so the motion's only job is to keep them steady and never get in the way of reading.
- **Step change:** no sliding carousel. Text and image crossfade in place over about 160 ms (opacity only), and the 16:9 box stays the same size on every step, About included, so the card never changes height and the Next button never moves under her finger. The dots keep their width change, on our cubic-bezier(0.23,1,0.32,1) and not the built-in `ease`.
- **Stale or empty box:** the fix is more than fading in. Preload and `decode()` all the step images when the card opens, and stack them in the box, showing only the current one. That way a fast Next swaps between pictures already in memory and can't show an old or blank frame. If one still isn't ready, the box keeps the same lilac background as the card, so it looks calm and doesn't look broken.
- **Pushback on today's About gem:** it bobs and twinkles forever. That breaks Amy's "no infinite motion" rule, and it moves right next to the one sentence we most want read. Let it rise in, bob twice, then rest. The card's overshoot curve (1.1) and the `ease`/`ease-out` lines also go, per the review house rule.
- **Hand-off into the Door:** today the modal disappears in a single frame, and the Halloween popup mounts in that same tick, which is the "two popups back to back" the playtest saw. Close with a 200 ms exit (the card sinks 8 px and fades, the scrim fades), timed with a plain timer and not animationend. Then the Door gets a quiet beat: nothing else animates in. I back Nathan on holding Halloween until after the first world visit. If Amy keeps it on the Door, it must wait at least ~600 ms after the exit so it reads as a new thing and not as a stutter.
- **Changed my mind:** at first I wanted the last step to point a glow at a world card. No: once `HINTS_ON` is flipped, the hints arrow owns "look here". A second pointer would compete with it, and if the hints stay OFF, a glow on the Door still teaches nothing about the world.
- **Reduced motion:** opacity-only crossfades and the exit fade, no translate or scale, the gem static, and the same preload. The timers don't change. **Settings entry:** the same motion, just starting at step 1. **Sound (for Wren):** I'd put no cue on Next, so a tap stays quiet. If Wren adds one, it lands on "Let's play" in time with the exit.
