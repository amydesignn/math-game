# Rowan, round 2 (engine, wiring, tests)

## Zoom
- **What it is:** the camera starts close behind her (`WORLD.camOffset [0,7,8]`, fov 42). Pinching (or scrolling on a computer) multiplies that distance between 0.55× and 2.2×. Zoom isn't saved, so every load starts at 1×. At 1×, a new kid sees her character, the cat and a few trees, and nothing to do. The playtest kid was stuck about 1.5 min until she zoomed out by accident.
- **Options:** (A) a How to Play line, "Pinch to see more". It costs nothing, but it describes a gesture before she's seen the world, and it's on a slide she skims. (B) **Widen the default.** One new knob, `WORLD.zoomStart`. Better: a pure `startZoom(aspect)` that starts wider on portrait (iPad and phone are the worst case), plus a test. It needs no code in the pinch handler or Scene beyond reading the start value. (C) A zoom hint. It's one config entry: no target (docked pill), shows when she's calm, has walked, and no gem or station has been on screen for N seconds. It retires the first time zoom moves more than 15% (App's pinch/wheel handler calls `notify('zoomed')`). It needs one new ctx field (`goalOnScreen`, from Scene's existing projection) and copy per device ("Pinch" vs "Scroll"). It only helps once `HINTS_ON` is on.
- **I recommend B now, C later as the backup. Zoom stays out of How to Play.** B fixes the cause for every kid, needs no reading, and works with hints off.
- **Costs of B, honestly:** she gets smaller on screen (Amy picked the Sorcerer's size on 10-14, so Amy should look), and tap targets shrink. The station and garden hitboxes are oversized, so they should be fine. The hint arrows don't care, because they project live. The camera angle doesn't change, since zoom only scales the offset.
- **Pushback:** wider doesn't *guarantee* a gem on screen, because sparkles spawn anywhere in ±18. Before picking a number I'd measure it: across seeded spawns on every map, at 375 / 768 portrait / 1024, what % show a gem or station at 1×, 1.4× and 1.8×? (Job 3 lesson: get the "how often" number first.) If even 1.8× is often empty, the parked guided first station is the real fix, and B only helps.
