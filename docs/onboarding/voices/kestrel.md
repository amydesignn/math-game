# Kestrel (Pond crew, Explorer): the About screen + truth check

- **About, for the kid (2 lines, ~16 words):** "Solve math to earn gems 💎. Build your world and grow a garden." / "Get one wrong? We'll show you how, step by step." This is the Atlas's strongest true claim (#2 recovery: `MathPopup.jsx` ask → recover, a worked example on a *similar* problem), and it's the same message Heron sells. I'd lead with the loop rather than "gym for your brain": the gym line is a parent metaphor, and a kid can't picture what it means for them. Fine as a small title if Amy loves it, but not as the main line.
- **Parent small print, true today:** "× ÷ + practice for grades 4–5 · no account needed." Pushback: **grade 6 isn't live yet.** `DIV_PHASE = 1` means 2-digit ÷ (G5); the 3-digit G6 band is a manual flip. So "4–6" overclaims today; say "4–5", or "4–6" only after Phase 2 ships. Also, a "7-year-old" reader is Grade 2. Either the reading target is low on purpose (fine, keep it) or the grade line confuses parents; Amy should pick one story. Don't name Prodigy or Animal Crossing in-game ("cozy, not fighting" is a marketing comparison, not a kid line), and no "I ace it!", no streaks, no "coming soon".
- **How to Play claims, TRUE per Atlas + code:** Sorcerer + cat, swap 3 free heroes in the Gem Shop (#6, `HEROES`) ✅ · tap a sparkle to solve a problem / quest stations give bonus gems (#1, #13; StationPopup completion bonus) ✅ · gems build things you place (#5) ✅ · 1 free seed, then 1 per 20 lifetime gems (`GARDEN.seedEveryGems: 20`) ✅ · garden in Sunny Town, water with gems, bloom, pick → 🧺 basket (#9–10; `waterCost: 5`, `waterings: 3`) ✅.
- **Flags:** (1) "If you struggle, it **calms you down**" is unverified. The game says "No worries" and then teaches; it doesn't calm anyone. Write "it shows you how, step by step." (2) "Unlock more heroes **and companions**" is true (Sproutling 💎50), but don't print a price: 50 is a placeholder. (3) "Sparkling gem" vs in-world "Snack Time": the kid sees a teal diamond in a ring, so the image should show that ring, or the words won't connect. (4) Say "the garden in **Sunny Town**", never just "the garden": Rosy Garden is a decoy (Atlas friction #2).
- **What a slide can't fix:** zoom-out is the real blocker (no station at spawn on iPad). If `HINTS_ON` stays off, I'd add a 4-word line to step 1, "Pinch to see more", rather than a shop line. Nathan's 🛍️ worry is real but second.
- **Images:** Oscar's step 3 (Merry Market) and step 4 (sparkle trail) don't match the new steps. The garden step needs a new capture, and the Atlas Thu shot (bed in bud → bloom) is that frame. Halloween: hold it until after the first world visit. It's a better shot and a better first minute.
