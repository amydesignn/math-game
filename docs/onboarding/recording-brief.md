# Recording brief for Amy: How to Play clips (draft for Linden + Fern to check)

**Format: send us plain screen recordings** (.mov or .mp4, any size). Please don't make GIFs. We cut and encode each clip the same way as the Halloween promo: a 720p WebM + MP4, muted, looping, plus a still poster image. A 6-second GIF at this size is about 5–10 MB. The video version is about 300 KB and looks sharper. With reduced motion turned on, the kid sees the still poster instead.

**How to record**
- Use the **iPad in landscape** (or a desktop browser window at about 1280×720). The card's image box is 16:9, so a portrait recording would get cropped hard.
- **Keep the HUD visible** (🛍️, 🧺, 💎). Kids match the icon in the clip to the button on screen, which is why we can't use canvas grabs.
- Please play in a **fresh guest** world, not Ivy's save. Gems: on `npm run dev`, type `__award(40)` in the console. Or just play a few problems; we'll cut the waiting out.
- Move slowly and on purpose: one tap, a short pause, the next tap. We'll speed up or trim the slow parts.
- About 10–20 s of raw footage per clip is plenty. Each final clip ends up about 5–8 s.

**The three clips**
1. **Meet your hero.** Show the Sorcerer and the cat taking a few steps (tap-to-walk). Tap 🛍️, then Heroes, and tap a free hero (e.g. Sunny). Close the shop: she's now the new hero.
2. **Earn gems, then build.** Walk into a glowing gem. Solve the problem; an easy one is best. The gems fly to the 💎 counter. Tap 🛍️, buy one build item, place it, then ✓. *(If Amy says yes to the zoom line, start with a slow pinch-out so a gem comes into view.)*
3. **Grow a garden.** Go to Sunny Town, walk to the garden bed and tap Plant. Water it until it blooms; we'll speed up the three waterings. Tap the flowers so the flower flies into the 🧺. End on the 🧺 count ticking up.

The About screen needs no recording; it keeps the gem stage.
