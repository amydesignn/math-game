Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Luxi Math (“we,” “us,” “our”) is a browser-based math learning game at math.luxi.land, made for children and families. We care about children’s privacy, and we’ve built Luxi to collect as little as possible. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. It’s written for the parents and guardians who manage accounts.

Our commitment to children’s privacy (COPPA)

We comply with the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from a child under 13 without verifiable parental consent.

  • A child can play Luxi without an account, and we collect no personal information at all — their progress is saved only on their own device.
  • Accounts are created and managed by a parent or guardian, using the parent’s email. A child cannot create an account on their own. When a parent creates an account, that is the parent’s consent for us to save the child’s game progress to it.

What we collect

When a parent creates an account:

  • The parent’s email address — used to send a one-time sign-in link and to identify the saved game. There is no password.
  • The child’s game progress — gems, level, the items they own, and the world they build. This is game data, not personal information about the child.

When playing as a guest (no account):

  • Nothing is collected. Progress is saved only on that device (in the browser) and is never sent to us.

For everyone:

  • Anonymous usage stats — privacy-friendly analytics (via Vercel) that count visits without cookies and without identifying anyone.

We do NOT collect a child’s name, age or date of birth, photo, voice, location, contacts, phone number, passwords, or payment details. Luxi has no purchases, and we do not process payments.

How we use what we collect

  • The parent’s email is used only to send sign-in links and to save the child’s progress across devices. We do not send marketing email, and we never share it for advertising.
  • Game progress is stored so the child doesn’t lose their gems or the world they’ve built, and can pick up on another device.
  • Usage stats help us see how many people play and improve the game. They are aggregate and don’t identify anyone.

Parental consent and control

Accounts are parent-managed. Creating an account (entering the parent’s email and opening the sign-in link) is how a parent consents to us saving their child’s progress. Because a child can play fully as a guest with no account, no personal information is ever collected from a child directly.

A parent or guardian can, at any time:

  • See what we hold — an email address and a saved game.
  • Delete the account and all of its progress.
  • Stop any further collection — sign out and play as a guest instead.

To do any of these, email hello@luxi.land.

Who we share data with

We never sell or rent your information, and we never share it for advertising. We share data only with the service providers that run the game for us, and only so they can provide their service:

  • Supabase — stores accounts and saved games.
  • Vercel — hosts the game and provides the cookieless analytics above.
  • The email service that sends your one-time sign-in link.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the safety and rights of our users.

How we keep it safe

  • Information is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and stored with reputable providers that encrypt data at rest.
  • Each account can only access its own saved game.
  • We keep the amount of data we hold deliberately small — the best protection is not collecting things in the first place.

Cookies and local storage

  • We use your browser’s local storage to keep guest progress, remember that a parent is signed in, and hold settings.
  • We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies, and our analytics is cookieless — so there’s no tracking-consent banner to click.

Keeping and deleting data

  • Guest progress lives only on the device until you clear it (clear the site data for math.luxi.land in the browser). We never have a copy.
  • Account data (the parent’s email and the saved game) is kept while the account is active. When you ask us to delete it, we remove it within 30 days.

Your rights, wherever you live

Whatever your location — including U.S. states with privacy laws such as California, and other countries — you can contact us to review or delete the information we hold. We don’t sell or share personal information, and we won’t treat you differently for exercising your rights.

Changes to this policy

If we make meaningful changes to what we collect or how we use it, we’ll update the date above, make it noticeable in the game, and — because accounts are parent-managed — notify account parents by email.

Contact

Questions, concerns, or want your data deleted? Email us at hello@luxi.land.
